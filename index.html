SAP B1 Finance Portal

No SAP B1 licenses for your finance team? No issue.

How we work

From first call to go-live

Everything happens remotely: calls, screen sharing, and access to your server over VPN or remote desktop.

  1. Call

    We talk through your process and what isn't working. You show me the screens.

  2. Scope

    You get a written scope with features, timeline and price before work starts.

  3. Build

    I build and test on a copy of your company database, never on live data.

  4. Test & train

    Your users test it, I fix what they find, and I train them.

  5. Go-live

    We switch to live data together, and I stay on for support.
